Biography & Background

Designing systems, tuning thermals, and building homelabs.

Hi, I’m Caleb. I am an Information Technology student in South Australia completing my Certificate III in IT at TAFE. My focus centers on custom computer hardware architecture, system assembly, network administration, and homelab infrastructure.

Over the past few years, I have planned, built, and maintained four custom personal computers along with a dedicated multi-node home server environment. Whether building a stealth gaming desktop with high-contrast interior components or managing low-power server nodes running containerized services, I prioritize clean aesthetics, optimal airflow, and reliable thermal engineering.

Homelab & Systems Administration

My homelab consists of compact HP ProDesk nodes running Linux, containerized with Docker and Portainer. I self-host local services including automated file synchronization (Syncthing), Immich photo management, Jellyfin media delivery, and dedicated multiplayer game servers. Everything is connected through a Tailscale mesh VPN, enabling secure, encrypted remote access without exposing ports to the public internet.

Creative Work & Other Pursuits

Beyond computer hardware and servers, I have a deep interest in aerial cinematography and digital filmmaking using my DJI Mini 4 Pro drone—practicing deliberate camera framing, neutral density filter exposure, and timeline editing in Adobe Premiere Pro. I also enjoy automotive mechanics and Japanese domestic market (JDM) car culture.
