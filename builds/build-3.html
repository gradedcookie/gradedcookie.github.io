Custom Desktop Build #03 Enthusiast Aesthetic

Hayley's White AIO Showcase

An all-white full-tower computing rig pairing a 360mm liquid AIO with circular pump display, white graphics card, and clean multi-fan pastel ambient lighting.

Chassis interior: 360mm top radiator, circular illuminated AIO pump display, and side intake array
1 / 3

Design Vision & Color Coordination

Hayley’s system was built to combine high thermal headroom with an immaculate white visual presentation. Every visible component—from the chassis frame and radiator to the fan frames and memory heatspreaders—was matched to create a cohesive all-white build.

The focal point of the interior is the circular illuminated AIO liquid pump block, which features a custom digital dot-matrix ring accent. This is surrounded by multi-zone pastel backlighting that gives the interior a bright, welcoming ambiance.

Cooling Topology & Air Balance

  • Side-Mounted Intake Cluster: Three 120mm intake fans positioned on the side tray draw fresh air directly into the main cavity, keeping the motherboard VRMs and graphics card supplied with cold air.
  • Top 360mm Radiator Exhaust: A high-fin-density 360mm radiator spans the entire roof of the case, extracting heat efficiently with three high-static-pressure fans.
  • Motherboard Diagnostics: The premium white motherboard includes an onboard seven-segment debug display and reinforced steel PCIe slots for secure GPU support.

Acoustics & Usability

With generous internal volume and seven PWM fans spinning at sub-800 RPM during everyday workloads, the system operates virtually inaudibly while maintaining component temperatures well below thermal limits.
