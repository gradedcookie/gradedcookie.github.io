Custom Desktop Build #02 Showcase Commission

Josh's Panoramic AERO Rig

A dual-chamber panoramic glass showpiece featuring an ice-blue aesthetic, Gigabyte RTX 4070 Ti SUPER AERO graphics card, 360mm ICE liquid cooler, and dedicated bottom intake cooling.

Completed build: Panoramic glass chassis with ice-blue lighting and Gigabyte RTX 4070 Ti SUPER AERO OC
1 / 2

Concept & Aesthetic Direction

Josh’s build was envisioned as a seamless, pillarless showcase machine. The dual-chamber layout relocates the power supply and storage behind the motherboard tray, leaving the main chamber exclusively for the high-end core components.

An all-white theme was paired with the silver and white brushed accents of the Gigabyte AERO series, illuminated by subtle ice-blue lighting to maintain a sophisticated, crisp look without overwhelming rainbow effects.

Thermal Routing & Cable Dressing

  • Chimney Airflow Architecture: Three 120mm bottom intake fans feed ambient room air directly into the GPU heatsink, while the top-mounted 360mm Gigabyte GAMING ICE radiator exhausts CPU thermal dissipation out the roof.
  • Custom 12VHPWR Power Cabling: The high-power PCIe 12VHPWR cable for the 4070 Ti SUPER was dressed with custom white cable combs to ensure zero bend stress on the high-current GPU connector.
  • High-Efficiency Power Delivery: Powered by a Corsair RM850e fully modular power supply, maintaining quiet operation and high electrical efficiency across demanding rendering and gaming sessions.

Performance Summary

With 16GB of high-speed VRAM and Ada Lovelace architecture, this system effortlessly drives high-frame-rate 1440p and 4K gaming with ray tracing and DLSS 3 enabled, all while maintaining low thermals under heavy loads.
