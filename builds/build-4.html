Custom Desktop Build #04 Friend Build

Tyson's White Gaming Rig

A clean, high-value white gaming PC assembled for Tyson, featuring a Gigabyte B550M K motherboard, AMD Ryzen processor, Sapphire Pure Radeon graphics, and custom white braided cabling.

Completed build: White mid-tower with magenta lighting, Sapphire Pure Radeon GPU, and braided white cable extensions
1 / 2

Project Goal & Collaboration

This build was assembled for my good friend Tyson, aiming to deliver exceptional 1080p and 1440p gaming performance within a smart, sensible budget while maintaining a clean, cohesive white aesthetic.

We paired the reliable Gigabyte B550M K AM4 motherboard with an AMD Ryzen processor and an all-white Sapphire Pure Radeon graphics card. The white shroud and illuminated red/magenta "SAPPHIRE" emblem complement the white chassis interior.

Attention to Detail & Cable Management

  • Bench Testing & Verification: Before mounting the hardware into the chassis, the CPU, cooler, and white T-Force memory were seated on the motherboard box to verify POST and update to the latest stable BIOS.
  • Custom Sleeved Extensions: Custom white 24-pin ATX and 8-pin PCIe cable extensions with black combs were installed to eliminate unsightly factory black PSU cables from the visible chamber.
  • Dynamic Airflow & Lighting: Dual top exhaust fans and a rear exhaust fan illuminated in deep magenta create positive airflow while accentuating the white chassis lines.

Gaming & Daily Usability

The system delivers fluid, high-FPS gameplay across multiplayer esports titles and open-world adventures. The compact micro-ATX platform keeps the overall desk footprint modest while maintaining low operating temperatures.
